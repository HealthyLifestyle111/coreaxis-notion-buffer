Questions Worth Asking Before Choosing a Menopause Program

The right program should recognize that you are not a template, a symptom list, or a set of macronutrient targets. Before enrolling, it is worth understanding what the program will actually consider—and what it may leave untouched.

Review the essential questions
  1. Will the program investigate my individual biology, history, life stage, and changing needs—or place me into a predetermined protocol?
  2. Is the nutrition genuinely curated for me, or is it a standard meal plan with adjusted calories, protein, and macronutrients?
  3. Does the program look beyond weight loss and symptom management to consider metabolic health, cellular function, nutrient status, stress physiology, sleep, digestion, hormones, recovery, and healthy aging?
  4. Will someone explore why my body may be responding differently now, or will I simply be coached to try harder at the same strategies?
  5. Does the program address prevention and long-term vitality, or only the concerns I report today?
  6. Are functional assessments, laboratory options, health history, wearable data, and other forms of personalization available when appropriate?
  7. If supplements, peptides, hormones, or medications are discussed, are they part of a broader strategy—or presented as the entire solution?
  8. Will the program evolve as my biology, results, and priorities change?
  9. Am I receiving personal guidance from someone who understands the whole picture, or primarily accessing videos, templates, group calls, and automated check-ins?
  10. Is there a clear distinction between education, wellness guidance, nutrition support, and licensed medical care?
  11. Will I understand why each recommendation is being made and how it connects to my longer-term health?
  12. Does the company clearly explain what is personalized, what is standardized, and what is not included?

You should not have to fit yourself into a menopause program. The program should be capable of meeting you.

Use these questions when considering any menopause program—including ours.
