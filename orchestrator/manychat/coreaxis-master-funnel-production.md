# CoreAxis — ManyChat Master Funnel Production

Status: A+ SALES/CONVERSION BUILD — CAMPAIGNS OFF EXCEPT EXPLICIT LAUNCH

## Business objective
Turn attention into qualified conversations, qualified conversations into purchases/activations, purchases into practitioner/client relationships, and clients into appropriate continuation/repeat revenue. Optimize for qualified revenue and customer lifetime value, not follower count or raw DM volume.

## Governing model
Traffic from Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, X, LinkedIn, Pinterest, website, paid ads, referrals and email enters the CoreAxis acquisition system. ManyChat is the conversation and conversion layer wherever native channel support exists. Non-native traffic uses tracked CTAs into a ManyChat-compatible entry point or owned destination. Zapier is the automation plumbing. Whole Practice is the practitioner/client environment. Wix is the owned website/shop/digital-product destination.

## Conversion architecture
ATTENTION -> DM/CTA -> QUALIFICATION -> ONE BEST ROUTE -> OFFER/DESTINATION -> PURCHASE OR ACTIVATION -> CLIENT ONBOARDING -> FULFILLMENT -> REASSESSMENT -> CONTINUATION/REACTIVATION.

## DM sales rules
- High-intent leads get the shortest path to the approved purchase/activation destination.
- Curious leads receive useful information plus a clear next step.
- Every incomplete journey gets an appropriate follow-up.
- Clicked-but-not-started and started-but-not-completed are recoverable sales states.
- Converted leads immediately leave acquisition sequences.
- Human handoff is triggered by clinical complexity, objections requiring judgment, or explicit high-intent request for a person.
- Never make the prospect sort through the entire catalog.

## Universal DM states
NEW -> ENGAGED -> QUALIFYING -> QUALIFIED -> ROUTED -> RECOMMENDATION GIVEN -> LINK SENT -> CLICKED -> STARTED -> CONVERTED -> FULFILLMENT -> REASSESSMENT -> CONTINUATION
Exceptions: NURTURE, NO RESPONSE, CONCIERGE, NOT APPROPRIATE, REACTIVATION.

## Lead magnets
Reusable families: menopause, whole-woman wellness, fertility/preconception, men's vitality, pet wellness, cellular wellness, peptides. Each requires asset, trigger, delivery, qualification, route, follow-up.

## Zapier contract
ManyChat event -> identify/create/update lead -> preserve source/campaign/intent/route -> Whole Practice or appropriate destination -> notification where needed -> conversion event -> suppression/update. Prevent duplicate records.

## Compliance routing
Automation must not diagnose, guarantee outcomes, invent protocols/products, or provide practitioner-level individualized clinical conclusions. Route complex/clinical questions to human review. After conversion, stop acquisition messaging.

## A+ QA gate
A campaign is not A+ until it passes trigger, qualification, routing, destination, purchase/activation, downstream-record, follow-up, suppression, attribution, and exception/handoff tests. Measure qualified conversion and revenue, not vanity engagement alone.

## Launch control
Only explicitly activated campaigns go live. All other campaign automations remain OFF/ready. EllieMD is the current launch candidate. Master architecture is reusable across future campaigns.
