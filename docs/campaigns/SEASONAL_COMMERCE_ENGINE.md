# CoreAxis Seasonal Commerce Engine

## Operating rule

Seasonal opportunities are layered onto the evergreen CoreAxis content engine. They do not replace the three campaign architecture.

## Immediate campaign: Labor Day 2026

Window: Aug 29–Sep 7, 2026.

Primary commercial source: the live Fullscript practitioner account. Use only the exact promotion, dates, eligibility and discount displayed in the account. Never invent a promotion code or discount.

CoreAxis execution sequence:

1. Verify current Fullscript promotion eligibility/activation.
2. Select a small curated collection aligned to the promotion and current CoreAxis education.
3. Produce one education-first announcement, one short-form video, one story sequence, one email, one DM response and one final-day reminder.
4. Link directly to the applicable Fullscript collection/storefront; do not route a retail promotion through an unrelated assessment.
5. Preserve CoreAxis disclosures and avoid medical/disease claims.
6. After the promotion, send replenishment/adherence education rather than continuing sale language.

## Recurring seasonal calendar

Labor Day → fall reset → back-to-routine → immune-season education → holiday travel → holiday gifting → New Year reset → winter wellness → spring reset → travel season → summer → back-to-school.

## Pulse integration

The Pulse Feed supplies current events, platform conversations, seasonal hooks, research developments and consumer questions. Each seasonal campaign should be refreshed from Pulse before production rather than recycled blindly.

## Commerce boundaries

Fullscript discounts apply to eligible supplement products; current Fullscript provider documentation states lab tests and services are not affected by supplement discount programs. Fullscript-run patient promotions are account/eligibility dependent.

## Low-friction reuse

One approved concept should produce: short video, caption, story, email, podcast segment, educational article/community post and DM response where appropriate. The message changes by channel; the factual claim and compliance standard do not.
