# CoreAxis Monday–Sunday Content Engine

Daily operating cadence: six content moments. The emotional state changes by time of day; the topic changes by audience need. One idea is repurposed across video, social, email, podcast and community rather than six unrelated ideas.

## Monday — Trust / discernment
06:30 — Calm authority: what a provider should review before a peptide conversation.
09:00 — Curiosity: the questions behind peptide trends.
11:30 — Education: evidence, formulation, source, monitoring, clinician oversight.
14:00 — Integrity: why disclosure and disciplined claims matter.
17:30 — Invitation: provider-reviewed options and pathway fit.
20:30 — Reflection: what are you actually trying to improve?

## Tuesday — Recognition / relief
06:30 — You are not imagining that your needs changed.
09:00 — Education: life stage changes the wellness question.
11:30 — Women: whole-woman context before isolated solutions.
14:00 — Men: recovery, vitality and longevity as one picture.
17:30 — Pets: individualized companion care.
20:30 — Household: coordinated care without collapsing everyone into one plan.

## Wednesday — Authority / evidence
06:30 — Research pulse: translate one current finding into plain language.
09:00 — Education: what evidence can and cannot tell you.
11:30 — Compliance education: what responsible wellness marketing sounds like.
14:00 — Labs: when testing adds useful information and when it does not.
17:30 — Provider review: what personalization actually means.
20:30 — Podcast/community question: what should we investigate next?

## Thursday — Possibility / action
06:30 — Small action: one high-value wellness question to answer today.
09:00 — Pomegranate/whole-woman education.
11:30 — Menopause Core education.
14:00 — Oyster/men’s vitality education.
17:30 — OVO/fertility education with licensed-provider boundary.
20:30 — Low-friction next step: education, assessment or provider-reviewed pathway.

## Friday — Confidence / curation
06:30 — What not to buy just because it is trending.
09:00 — Fullscript curation: one educational product category, no unsupported claims.
11:30 — CellCore education: foundation, assessment, practitioner review; never bypass intake.
14:00 — Supplement literacy: quality, dose, fit, interactions and monitoring.
17:30 — One protocol is not one-size-fits-all.
20:30 — Weekend question / community prompt.

## Saturday — Connection / lifestyle
08:00 — Food and daily rhythm.
10:30 — Movement and recovery.
13:00 — Sleep and nervous-system support.
15:30 — Pet + household wellness.
18:00 — Behind-the-scenes practitioner philosophy.
20:30 — Podcast/community conversation.

## Sunday — Reflection / readiness
08:00 — Weekly reflection.
10:30 — What changed this week?
13:00 — What is still unresolved?

18:00 — Next-week Pulse preview.
20:30 — Soft invitation to begin with the appropriate CoreAxis gateway.

## Production rule
Each slot receives: one hook, one factual teaching point, one CTA, one destination, one attribution code, and the applicable compliance/disclosure treatment. Never create a claim merely to fill a slot.
