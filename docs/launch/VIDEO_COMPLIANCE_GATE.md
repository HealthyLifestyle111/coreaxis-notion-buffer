# CoreAxis Video Compliance Gate

A video cannot enter the publishing lane until all checks pass.

1. Claim is educational and supportable.
2. No diagnosis, cure, guaranteed outcome, or disease-treatment promise.
3. Provider-reviewed boundary is preserved where treatment/provider content is discussed.
4. Affiliate, paid, gifted, or other material connection is disclosed clearly in the content itself when applicable.
5. Disclosure is visible/audible without requiring the viewer to open a description.
6. AI-generated or significantly altered media is labeled according to the destination platform's current rules.
7. CTA is singular and points to the current approved destination.
8. No obsolete URL, offer, pricing, or program name is introduced during editing.
9. No private health information is shown.
10. Caption, on-screen text, spoken audio and thumbnail tell the same factual story.
11. Final edit preserves the approved hook and required disclosure rather than adding unsupported claims for engagement.
12. The asset is approved before automatic publication.

FTC principle: a material financial or other relationship with a brand must be disclosed clearly and conspicuously with the endorsement itself. Do not rely solely on a profile disclosure or a hidden description.

Source: FTC Disclosures 101 for Social Media Influencers.
