# CoreAxis Email Lifecycle QA — Monday Release

## Entry

Every new lead receives exactly one welcome message. Attribution persists from source through intake. Health answers never enter marketing fields.

## Acquisition nurture

Run the approved campaign sequence only while lead status is pre-conversion and opted in. Duplicate entry must not restart the welcome sequence.

## Conversion

When a qualified lead converts or enters delivery/partner onboarding, acquisition nurture stops. No acquisition CTA should continue after conversion.

## Unsubscribe

Unsubscribe immediately suppresses marketing nurture. Transactional/service messages remain separate.

## Pathway tests

Foundation → Whole Practice → practitioner review → appropriate next pathway.

Pomegranate → OVO when fertility/preconception intent is identified; Pomegranate → Menopause Core when midlife transition is identified.

Oyster → OVO when fertility becomes the primary goal.

Healthier Together → individual human pathways + Vibrant Pets where applicable.

Vibrant Pets → VDI Labs where indicated; household coordination → Healthier Together.

CellCore → Whole Practice first; no direct social bypass.

PEPTIDE → direct EllieMD professional/provider-reviewed route using the corrected referral destination.

## Email content standards

One primary CTA per message. Current destination only. No obsolete pricing. No diagnosis, treatment guarantee, disease-treatment claim, or implication that CoreAxis itself prescribes. Affiliate/financial relationship disclosures must be clear when applicable.

## Seasonal commerce

When a live Fullscript patient promotion is active, use only the exact account-confirmed promotion details. Fullscript-run promotions are separate from the ordinary dispensary discount and are limited by regional discount rules. Labs/services are not discounted by supplement promotions.
